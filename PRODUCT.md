# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS (user choice, no build step, easy to host e.g. on GitHub Pages)

## Users

Primary users are prospective clients of a personal trainer: people looking to start or commit to strength/fitness training, browsing a trainer's site to evaluate credibility, offer, pricing, and to book a first session or consultation. Secondary "user" is the personal trainer themself, who will reuse this as a starting template and swap in real copy/photos/pricing for their own business.

## Product Purpose

A landing-page template for a personal trainer's business website. It exists to build trust and convert visitors into leads/bookings (trial session, consultation, sign-up). Success = a visually striking, credible, conversion-oriented template that a trainer can drop their own content into with minimal rework.

## Positioning

This is explicitly a reusable **template**, not a bespoke site for a named trainer — differentiated by an intentionally bold, aggressive "TESTOSTERON" visual identity (raw/industrial gym aesthetic) rather than the generic soft-wellness look most trainer sites default to.

## Operating Context

Single static multi-section page (no backend): hero, about the trainer, offer/training packages, transformations (before/after), testimonials, pricing, contact/sign-up form. The contact form has no backend wired up in this template (placeholder submit / mailto or a clearly marked TODO for real integration).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No CMS/backend; content is hardcoded HTML meant to be edited directly.
- No real trainer identity, brand name, logo, or photos exist yet — this is a generic/placeholder template.
- No real testimonials, before/after images, or pricing data exist; all such content must be clearly fictional/placeholder, not presented as real claims.
- Must be responsive (mobile/tablet/desktop).

## Brand Commitments

None — no existing name, logo, or brand assets. A placeholder trainer/brand name may be used but must read as obviously a placeholder.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real photos, before/after images, or testimonials are available. All imagery/photos in this build are placeholders (generated or illustrative), and all testimonials/results are fictional sample content clearly meant for replacement.

## Product Principles

1. Bold over safe — the design should visually shout "TESTOSTERON": raw, high-contrast, industrial, unapologetically masculine energy.
2. Template-first — every section should be obviously and easily editable; keep structure simple and content clearly swappable.
3. Conversion-oriented — every section should ladder toward a CTA (book a session / join now).
4. Honesty about placeholders — no fabricated claims dressed as real, verifiable facts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard web accessibility expected (sufficient contrast despite the dark/bold palette, keyboard-navigable nav and form, alt text on images).
